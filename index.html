ABOUT SEA AUTO

Turning Engineering Ideas Into Practical Possibilities.

SEA AUTO is an emerging technology initiative based in Begusarai, Bihar, India. It focuses on exploring and developing technology-driven concepts in automation, electronics, Internet of Things, AgriTech and robotics.

Started as a student project in 2022, SEA AUTO continues to explore practical engineering ideas through experimentation, prototype development and technology education.

Our approach is centred on learning, innovation and creating solutions that address real-world challenges.
