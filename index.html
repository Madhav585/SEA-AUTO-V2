Our Story

SEA AUTO started in 2022 as a student initiative with an interest in electronics, technology and automation. What began as a learning project has grown into an effort to explore practical solutions for everyday challenges.

Based in Begusarai, Bihar, we work around ideas in smart agriculture, IoT devices, robotics, electronics and STEM education. Our approach is simple: understand a problem, experiment with technology and build working prototypes.

We aim to make technology learning accessible and encourage students to turn their ideas into practical projects.
